Chassis physical ports are unlabeled. All 8 physical ports on the Chassis node (PP 1-8, connecting to PCIe Card Adapter, PCIe Card, Motherboard, HDD drive, External Interface, Power Supply, Environment, and Stand) currently use the generic auto-generated "PP N" naming with no descriptive label. Recommend renaming each port to reflect what it connects to (e.g. "PP - PCIe Adapter", "PP - Power Supply") for readability and easier traceability in diagrams and fabrics.
