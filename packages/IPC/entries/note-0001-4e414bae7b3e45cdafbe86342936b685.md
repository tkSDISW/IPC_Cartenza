Started the IPC package in the Cartenza knowledge repo to track observations, decisions, and lessons learned for the SIMATIC IPC Capella model (repo: tkSDISW/IPC, branch 2506).

Initial model review (via capella-fabric):
- verify_model ran clean across OA, SA, LA, and PA phases — no missing names, no unallocated functions.
- Reviewed the PA layer in detail via the "5 [PAB] Nodes with Requirements" diagram fabric, focusing on the PCIe Card Adapter: it connects to the Motherboard via one electrical (EL) and one mechanical (M) physical link, to the Chassis via a mechanical (ML) link carrying the "Exp CS" component exchange (Expansion Heat), and to the PCIe Card via an electrical (EL) link. Its allocated behavior (Expansion System) covers Receive PCIe Module and Deliver PCIe.
- Also catalogued the Chassis node's 8 physical ports (mounts to Motherboard, PCIe Card Adapter, PCIe Card, HDD drive, Power Supply, Stand; a physical link to External Interface; and an environmental port dumping heat to Environment).

Naming/data-quality notes worth follow-up: duplicate "Environment" components at both LA and PA layers, a placeholder-named component "LA 10" at both layers, and several spelling inconsistencies ("Facilitiy", "Maintence", "Expasion System", "UDB 3").
